THE MEMORY VAULT

We didn't know these moments would matter.

That's the funny thing about memories. You don't know you're making one until you look back and realize you wouldn't trade it for anything.

01
CHAPTER ONE

And then... you discovered spelling was optional.

Somewhere along the way, you decided that grammar and spelling were merely suggestions. Every conversation became a surprise test: "Is this actually a word, or did she just invent it?" But honestly, correcting your spelling became part of the friendship. At this point, autocorrect has probably given up on you too. 😭

02
CHAPTER TWO

Remembering things? Never heard of her.

You can remember the most random useless detail not useless but yeahhhh useless from three years ago, but ask you what we discussed five minutes ago and suddenly... system error. Plans, conversations, promises — everything disappears into the mysterious void known as your memory. 😭

03
CHAPTER THREE

Every problem has one solution. Biryani.

Somehow, every conversation eventually reaches the same conclusion: "Chal biryani khane chalte hai." Sad? Biryani. Bored? Biryani. Birthday? Biryani. Broke? ...still somehow planning biryani. 💀 At this point, we're not even sure if it's a friendship or a biryani subscription.

04
CHAPTER FOUR

And somehow... we're still here.

After the spelling disasters, forgotten conversations, unnecessary arguments and countless biryani plans... somehow, we're still talking. Still laughing. Still roasting each other. Still making completely unnecessary memories. And honestly, that's probably the best part. ❤️
